Flash News Article Link Fix

Analysis of why Flash News / Browse opened catholicatenews.in, what was fixed in local Strapi, and how to apply the same flash-news-only data fix to production. Frontend same-domain routing lives in the Next app; this Strapi project owns the data repair scripts and report.

1. Root cause analysis

Finding Detail
Schema flash-news-item has article (relation) and externalUrl (fallback when no article).
Imported data Flash rows had externalUrl → catholicatenews.in and article: null, even when matching articles already existed in Strapi (same titles).
Frontend bug (Next) Link builder preferred externalUrl over article slug (docs say the opposite). Fixed in mosc-temp getNewsHomePageData.ts.
Lifecycle pitfall flash-news-item lifecycle strips/auto-assigns tenant for admin editors only. API-token PUT of article can leave published rows without tenant.
Populate pitfall Article relation must point at the published article row. Linking a draft article id makes API populate=article return null.

2. What was fixed locally (flash news only)

  • Linked 5 mosc_malankara_orthodox_2 flash items to published articles by title (item 5 Malayalam Russia → English Russia article).
  • Repaired tenant on published flash rows via POST /api/migration/fix-published.
  • Ensured article link table points at published article ids so populate works.
  • Did not change articles body, categories, ads, or other content types as the intended ops scope.

Reference snapshot:

documentation/data/flash-news-article-links-local-snapshot.json

3. Scope rules for production

Flash news only. Do not create/delete articles, re-seed news, change categories, media, or tenants. Only update flash-news-items: set article relation + repair tenant on published rows. Keep externalUrl as historical reference; frontend prefers article when present.
